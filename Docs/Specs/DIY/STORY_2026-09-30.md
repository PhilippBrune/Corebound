# Story — concept and first draft, 2026-09-30

**Owner request:** *"Create as well a 'Story' concept and the 'Story' which the player wants to
follow. The focus is not super strong on the story but I would like to add some to the game."*

**Owner decisions (2026-09-30):** S1-2, S2-1, **S3-2**, S4-1, S5-1. **Built 2026-09-30** — see "As
built" at the end. With S3-2 the classes stay mechanical: the class bios below are not used.

The first half is **how** story gets into the game without getting in the way; the second half is
**the story itself**, with every line the game would show, in English and German.

---

## Part 1 — The concept

### Rules for a light story

1. **Never during combat.** Text appears only at moments the game already pauses its pressure:
   the stage arrival banner, the boss's 2-second arrival warning, the boss defeat notice, the run
   end, and menus. Vision §19.6 (readability) is not negotiable for lore.
2. **One or two lines at a time.** Every in-run line fits the existing banner or notice.
3. **The story explains the loop.** Death, restarts, meta progression and Ascension should all
   make sense inside the fiction — that is where a light story earns its place (Vision §19.12).
4. **The deeper story is optional.** It lives in *Core Logs* in the Records screen, unlocked by
   milestones the player reaches anyway. A player who never opens Records loses nothing.
5. **Everything is data.** Lines live on the assets that already describe the moment (stage
   reward data, boss data), with a `_xDe` sibling per the localisation rule.

### Where the text goes (all existing surfaces)

| Moment | Surface | Exists? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First New Run on a profile | Premise card before the run (three lines) | new, small |
| Stage arrival | Stage banner detail label (`StageRewardData.TransitionLabel`) | yes |
| Boss arrival | Boss HUD warning panel during the 2-s arrival | yes |
| Boss defeat | "Boss Defeated" notice, second line | yes |
| Run lost / won | Run end screen, one line under the title | yes |
| First full clear | Ending card (five lines), once per profile | new, small |
| Anytime | **Core Logs** tab in Records | new tab, reuses the Records list |

### Decisions

#### S1 — How much story?

1. **Minimal:** premise card, stage lines, boss lines, ending.
2. **Minimal plus Core Logs** in Records, unlocked by milestones.
3. **Plus a speaking companion** during runs.

**Recommendation: 2.** The Logs carry the twist for players who want it, at the cost of one
Records tab; the run itself stays as lean as 1.

#### S2 — Who tells it?

1. **The Core speaks** — short, first-person lines. The thing you defend has a voice.
2. **A neutral narrator** — log entries in third person.
3. **Environment only** — no text, just names and art.

**Recommendation: 1.** Everything in the game is about protecting the Core; letting it talk turns
an HP bar into a relationship, and it is the cheapest way to make a death feel like something.

#### S3 — Should the classes (CHARACTER_CLASSES_2026-09-30) be part of the story?

1. **Yes:** each class is a different Binder frame with a two-line bio and one Log.
2. **No:** classes are mechanical only.

**Recommendation: 1.** It costs five paragraphs and gives each unlock a moment.

#### S4 — The ending

1. **One ending** after the first full clear, then Paragon framed as "the next cycle".
2. **Two endings**, the second after the first Ascension rank.
3. **No ending**; the loop is the story.

**Recommendation: 1.** A roguelite needs its first clear to feel like an arrival; the second
ending can come later if Ascension grows.

#### S5 — Premise card on every profile or only once?

1. **Once per profile**, skippable, replayable from Records.
2. **Every New Run.**

**Recommendation: 1.**

### What the code needs

- `_transitionLabelDe` / `_completionLabelDe` on `StageRewardData` (the English fields exist).
- `_arrivalLine`, `_defeatLine` (+ De) on `BossData`, shown by `BossHudPresenter` (now in the
  authored `OverlayCanvas` scene).
- `CoreLogData` assets (title, text EN/DE, unlock: quest id, boss id or level) and a Logs tab in
  Records next to Runs and Cards.
- A premise card and an ending card: one panel each, text only, reusing the Records panel frame.
- `_runLostLines` / `_runWonLine` somewhere global (the run end presenter picks one).

**Rough size:** half a day for data and wiring of the in-run lines; half a day for the Logs tab;
writing and German review on top.

## Part 2 — The story

### The premise (shown once, before the first run)

> **EN**
> The makers built the Works to rebuild a dead world, and put its last seed in a single Core.
> Then the makers were gone, and the machines of the Works forgot what they were for.
> You are a Binder. You are bound to the Core. If it breaks, it will bring you back — and you
> will try again.
>
> **DE**
> Die Erbauer schufen das Werk, um eine tote Welt neu aufzubauen, und legten ihren letzten Samen
> in einen einzigen Kern.
> Dann verschwanden die Erbauer, und die Maschinen des Werks vergaßen, wofür sie da sind.
> Du bist ein Binder. Du bist an den Kern gebunden. Wenn er zerbricht, holt er dich zurück — und
> du versuchst es erneut.

### The world in five sentences

The **Works** is a buried, planet-sized factory built to make a world. Its heart is the **Core**,
the last seed of the makers, which can only take root at the bottom, in the **Core Nexus**. The
Works' salvage machines — the **Scour** — no longer remember the blueprint and now strip
everything for parts, and a live Core is the richest part there is. The Core cannot defend itself,
so it makes **Binders**: frames bound to it, which it pulls back to the Foundry whenever it falls.
Each run is one descent; the Works rearranges its halls around the Core as it goes down, which is
why the floor changes under you between stages while the Core stays where it is.

### Why the loop makes sense

| Game system | In the story |
| --- | --- |
| Death and a new run | The Core pulls its Binder back to the Foundry and starts the descent again. |
| Scrap | What the Scour drop; spent in the moment. |
| Alloy (kept between runs) | Salvage the Core keeps when it falls back. |
| Skill Points and levels | What the Binder remembers from every cycle. |
| Towers | The Works' old defences, which answer to a Binder. |
| Paragon and Ascension | Cycles after the first seeding — see the ending. |
| Wardens (bosses, Relay Wardens) | Binders who failed too often and were taken by the Scour. |

### The descent — stage by stage

Each stage has an **arrival line** (stage banner), and its boss has an **arrival line** (the 2-s
warning) and a **defeat line** (under "Boss Defeated"). All lines are the Core speaking.

#### Stage 1 — The Foundry

Where Binders are made, and where every run begins. Its guardian still obeys an order from before
the makers left: *nothing leaves the Foundry*.

| | EN | DE |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | "The Foundry. You were made here. Keep me close." | „Die Gießerei. Hier wurdest du gemacht. Bleib bei mir." |
| Boss — **Corebreaker Warden** | "It still guards the doors. It thinks I am being stolen." | „Es bewacht noch die Tore. Es glaubt, ich werde gestohlen." |
| Defeat | "The doors are open. Down." | „Die Tore sind offen. Hinab." |

#### Stage 2 — Reactor Depths

The Works' power plant, still running with no one to use it. Its Sentinel has been starved for
fuel for a very long time.

| | EN | DE |

| Defeat | "The lights are going out behind us. Good." | „Hinter uns gehen die Lichter aus. Gut." |

#### Stage 3 — Salvage Fields

The Scour's nest: mountains of stripped machines. The **Salvage Overseer** decides what is parts

#### Stage 5 — Core Nexus

The bottom of the Works, where the Core can finally take root. It is guarded by the oldest Warden:

### The ending (first full clear, once per profile)

> **EN**
> The Core takes root in the Nexus, and for the first time in an age the Works is quiet.
> Far above, the halls begin to build what they were built to build.
> The Binder stays. Binders always stay.
> Then, somewhere deeper than the Nexus, a second Core wakes up — and calls your name.
> *The next cycle has begun.*
>
> **DE**
> Der Kern schlägt Wurzeln im Nexus, und zum ersten Mal seit einer Ewigkeit ist das Werk still.
> Weit oben beginnen die Hallen zu bauen, wofür sie gebaut wurden.
> Der Binder bleibt. Binder bleiben immer.
> Dann erwacht irgendwo tiefer als der Nexus ein zweiter Kern — und ruft deinen Namen.
> *Der nächste Zyklus hat begonnen.*

After the ending, **Paragon levels and Ascension** are the later cycles: each Ascension rank is a
deeper, older Core — which is why the game gets harder and why the Binder keeps growing.

## As built (2026-09-30)

- **All text lives in one asset,** `ScriptableObjects/Story/StoryData_Default` (English and German
  side by side), hung off `MetaProgressionDefinitionData`, rather than on the stage and boss assets
  as Part 1 proposed — one file to read and edit the whole story in.
- **The Core's voice** (S2-1) is a line low in the frame, in the authored `OverlayCanvas` scene
  (`CoreVoiceCanvas`). `StoryDirector` (attached by `WaveManager`) speaks at the start of every stage
  (run start, Continue, and each crossfaded stage change), when a boss arrives, when it falls, and
  when a run is lost (one of four lines). It never speaks in the tutorial. At most one line waits:
  the newest replaces an older one that has not been shown yet, because a boss can fall before the
  line about its arrival has faded.
- **Premise** (S5-1): a profile's first New Run opens on a premise page ("The Core"); Confirm turns
  to the weapon choice and marks it seen. It is also the first Core Log, so it can be read again.
- **Ending** (S4-1): at the first full clear the ending card (`EndingCanvas`) fades in over the Run
  Complete screen; the Core's line and the final relic reveal are held off it. Later wins get the
  Core's "Rooted again" line. Audio 24 (victory sting) plays on every win.
- **Core Logs** (S1-2): a tab in Records with 13 entries (the premise and the twelve logs). Titles
  are listed, locked ones as "???"; the selected log's text or its unlock hint shows under the list.
  German texts were written with the build.
- **Credits:** a fourth Records tab; Audio 25 plays while it is open. The credit lines are a draft
  for the owner to correct.
- Stage 1 is named the Foundry everywhere (Open Topics 5 (a)); every stage has a German name.

**Verified** with the autopilot on Profile 2: the premise page on a first New Run; the Foundry
arrival line during the spawn delay; the ending card alone on screen at the end of `boss1`/`boss5`
(panel alpha 1.00 at capture); Records with Core Logs 11 / 13 and the credits (new `records` variant).
The run-lost line was seen on the Run Failed screen of an Engineer probe ("I kept what I could.
Take it."). **Not verified:** the victory sting and the credits loop by ear (agents cannot hear),
and the German texts on screen.
